# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Product Summary
A simple web app that helps creators turn ambitious ideas into something feelable by connecting them with contributors who are both skilled and genuinely interested in the project.

## Problem
Many creators have exciting ideas, but they often feel they are too ambitious or too early to pursue them alone. The missing piece is not just technical skill—it is the confidence that the idea can become real by finding the right people to build with.

## Product Goal
Create a web app that makes an ambitious project feel possible by helping a creator:
- describe the idea clearly
- define what skills the project needs
- see contributors who match those skills
- understand whether they are interested in the idea
- connect with the right people quickly

## Target Users
1. Creators with early-stage or ambitious ideas
2. Contributors who want to join projects that match their interests and abilities

## Primary User Journey
1. Log in
2. Create a project
3. Add required skills and interests
4. Review matched contributors
5. See their fit and interest in the idea
6. Contact or invite them

## Core Product Requirements
- Creators can create a project
- Projects include:
  - title
  - short description
  - required skills
  - project interests or themes
- Contributors can create a profile
- Profiles include:
  - name
  - bio
  - skills
  - interests
- Matching logic considers:
  - skill overlap
  - interest in the idea
- Match results show:
  - contributor name
  - skill overlap
  - interest overlap
  - fit score
  - short profile summary
- Creators can review a candidate before contacting them
- Creators can connect or invite a contributor

## Product Experience
The product should make the creator feel:
- not alone
- more confident that the idea can happen
- more able to move from concept to team
- supported by concrete evidence of fit rather than vague interest

## Priority Features
1. Project creation
2. Contributor profile creation
3. Matching by skill and interest
4. Clear match summary
5. Connect/invite action

## Out of Scope
- Real-time chat
- Payment or profit splitting
- Complex ownership tracking
- Full project management features
- Production-grade authentication
- Large-scale networking features

## Success Criteria
The app is successful when a creator can:
- share an ambitious idea
- quickly understand what skills are needed
- see contributors who are a real fit
- feel confident that the project is possible with the right people
